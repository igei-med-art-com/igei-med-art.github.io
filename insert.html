IGEI-MED-ART

IGEI Inc.

IGEI is a project by physician and artist Ryoto Jinnai that seeks to merge medicine and art. Through works, design, creative direction, curated exhibitions, workshops, podcasts, and other creative outputs, IGEI “prescribes” new possibilities and explores the future of healthcare beyond conventional boundaries.
